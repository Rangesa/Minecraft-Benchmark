# MineBench compatibility matrix

The matrix records observed compatibility rather than claiming support from metadata alone.

| Minecraft | Loader | Adapter | Tier | Real launch | Autorun | Deep telemetry | External frametime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.21.1 | Fabric 0.19.5 | `mcbenchmark-fabric` | STANDARD | PASS | PASS | PASS | pending regression |

Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, and Quilt metadata detection exists in the Launcher. A detected environment is not promoted to a supported tier until the required real tests pass.

The current score profile is provisional. Ranking must remain disabled until the calibration and external frametime path are stable.
